1. What's proposed

A draft air permit adding 337 generators and 9 fire pumps, for 572 generators and 11 pumps, at 1435 Highway 54 West, Fayetteville, Fayette County. N1 D1 Proposed generator output is 546×2.5 + 26×1.25 = 1,397.5 MW, excluding pumps; this is nameplate capacity, not utility demand. N2 EPD's current draft table lists November 30, 2026; the older advisory says October 23. The collection links no revised deadline PDF. A3 Use the advisory's 4:30 p.m. Eastern receipt cutoff and Air-Permits.Comments@dnr.ga.gov, subject “Air permit application — 30027”; request the application/calculations from steve.allison2@dnr.ga.gov. A4 No application download was posted in that table.

2. What the permit would allow

13 separate rolling 12-month fuel ceilings, totaling 1,583,001 gallons arithmetically; each building group must comply separately. D2 D3 These replace the existing 670,000-gallon Title V restriction. N1 Fuel limits avoid the 250-ton/year threshold for federal preconstruction (PSD) review (40 CFR 52.21) and the state VOC rule; 236.11 tons/year NOx is modeled potential, not a direct NOx cap. The proposal needs a Title V operating permit, with an application required after startup. N4

Federal emergency hours have no numerical ceiling; maintenance/readiness ordinarily allows 100 hours/year with exceptions, including the conditional 50 non-emergency hours. D4 D5 The state pathway requires <500 total hours/generator/year, with 10 p.m.–4 a.m. routine testing, May–September; a fallback ozone-season NOx standard applies outside the definition. D5 D6 Unit hour meters, fuel tracking and monthly/per-group rolling fuel totals provide the compliance record. D7 D8

3. Where it sits in Georgia

Today's issued QTS limit remains 670,000 gallons, Title V avoidance, not the proposed larger allowances. The proposal changes regulatory category: it avoids PSD, not Title V. N4 Compare Microsoft CCO06's <249-ton PSD cap and Edged's 99-ton Title V cap in the facility table. Fuel gallons and NOx tons are different limit types.

4. Fuel-cap calculation

Full-load generator-hours and full-campus outage hours: unknown. The downloaded narrative provides ratings and fuel ceilings, but no documented full-load gallons/hour rate. N2 N3 The sum of group ceilings implies a conservative fuel-cap CO₂ illustration of 16,162 metric tonnes: 1,583,001×10.21÷1,000 using EPA's No. 2 distillate factor, PDF p.1. This is not an operating forecast, transferable fuel budget or legal CO₂ limit; overlapping draft group assignments also need correction. D3
