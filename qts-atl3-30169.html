Application 30169 • Comment brief • November 2, 2026. D = draft, N = EPD narrative, A = advisory; numbers are PDF pages, including covers.

1. What's proposed

A draft air permit for a new data center at 2883 Gordon Highway, Augusta, Richmond County, with 224 critical and 12 house diesel generators. D1 N1 Their derived total output is 224×2.5 + 12×1.25 = 575 MW of generator nameplate capacity, not utility demand. The narrative specifies Tier 2 engines without additional controls. N2 N1 EPD's current draft table lists December 3, 2026; the older advisory says October 16 and misspells Gordon as “Gorgon.” The collection links no revised deadline PDF. A3 Send comments by the notice's 4:30 p.m. Eastern cutoff to Air-Permits.Comments@dnr.ga.gov, subject “Air permit application — 30169.” Request the application/calculations from steve.allison2@dnr.ga.gov; no application download was posted in the table. A4

2. What the permit would allow

249 short tons NOx per rolling 12 months, one ton below the 250-ton threshold for federal preconstruction (PSD) review (40 CFR 52.21). D2 N3 This does not avoid the 100-ton Title V threshold: the narrative classifies the facility as a major source requiring a Title V operating permit, with an application within one year of generator startup. N3

Emergency operation has no numerical federal hour ceiling; maintenance/readiness ordinarily allows 100 hours/year, with exceptions. The conditional 50 non-emergency hours are included, not additional. D3 D4 No ozone-season night-testing window is specified for Richmond County; the narrative says the state engine rule requiring that pathway does not apply there. N5 Hour meters, at-least-hourly fuel and load records feed run-by-run calculations and monthly/rolling NOx totals. D5 D7 D8 20.75 tons in a month triggers notice within 15 days; it is not a monthly cap. The 249-ton rolling cap still applies. D8 D9

3. Where it sits in Georgia

The proposed 249-ton cap matches the PSD approach of Microsoft CCO06 and Equinix Atlanta; Amazon ATL089 permits 249.9 tons. These are different from Edged's 99-ton Title V cap. The facility table keeps issued baselines separate from proposals. No issued ATL3 baseline was found in the recovered corpus; the narrative describes a greenfield project. N1

4. Fuel-cap calculation

Fuel-derived generator-hours, full-campus outage hours and maximum CO₂ from a fuel ceiling: unknown. This draft limits NOx rather than annual fuel volume; the downloaded narrative supplies no full-load gallons/hour rates. D2 N2 Fuel monitoring alone does not establish a fuel cap. D5 For context, EPA's No. 2 distillate factor is 10.21 kg CO₂/gallon, PDF p.1; without capped gallons it cannot yield a cap-based annual CO₂ total.

5. Questions a comment could raise

  • What operational response keeps an extended outage within the rolling NOx allowance, beyond after-the-fact notification? D9
  • Were additional NOx controls evaluated? The narrative specifies none beyond Tier 2 certification, while Amazon ATL089's narrative describes SCR. N1

6. What we couldn't determine

The application, consumption curves, expected operating hours, revised-date PDF, and independently measured emissions. The narrative's public-review addendum still contains unfilled date/comment placeholders. N9
