What the pattern does and does not show

The issued corpus contains strict <25-ton legacy NOx limits, 99/99.9-ton Title V limits, 249/249.9-ton PSD limits, and fuel/hour restrictions. A strict “less than 25” condition sits below 25, not at it. The historical 25-ton county/NSR or Part 70 bases must not be assumed to be today's trigger. Low numerical caps alone do not prove the developer's motive. Threshold interpretations above follow the cited permit/narrative; unresolved current county-specific rules remain unknown.

Specific unresolved evidence

  • QTS Fayetteville: the issued amendment's cover says two additional pumps, while its operative condition names only FP001–FP002. Its equipment narrative also lists two total. Pump counts therefore follow the operative condition for the baseline and remain a discrepancy to clarify. The proposed draft assigns EG510–EG546 to DC14, overlapping DC13; its narrative instead assigns EG519–EG546. These source differences are not silently repaired.
  • Edged: the 2026 amendment changes certification/emergency-use conditions but leaves base condition 2.1's 99-ton cap intact. The old Tier 4/SCR description must not be carried forward as the current control requirement without checking the amendment.
  • Flexential: MW above follows the issued amended equipment list; the narrative lists different ratings for some existing units. Edgeconnex's amended group hours replace its former combined-energy restriction; the issued index lists the amendment although its PDF cover leaves issuance blank.
  • Travelers: the scanned PDFs were read visually. The amendment's header and concluding paragraph identify the matching base; one introductory base-reference number is inconsistent. Seek agency confirmation of that clerical discrepancy.
  • No additional issued documents were searched during M2. Missing amendments, scanned older documents and changes after this snapshot can affect a consolidation. The table supports comparisons within the recovered corpus.
