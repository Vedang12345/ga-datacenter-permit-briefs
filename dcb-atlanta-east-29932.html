1. What's proposed

A draft air permit for 97 additional diesel generators, 103 total, at 1726 Farmer Road, Conyers, Rockdale County. The narrative's mixed fleet is 79×3 MW + 24×2.5 MW = 297 MW, calculated nameplate output, not utility demand. D1 N2 N3 Comments are due November 30, 2026, 4:30 p.m. Eastern, to Air-Permits.Comments@dnr.ga.gov, subject “Air permit application — 29932.” Request the application and supporting calculations from steve.allison2@dnr.ga.gov; no application download was posted in the draft collection. A3

2. What the permit would allow

736,501 diesel gallons per rolling 12 months, including emergency use, to stay below the 100-short-ton/year threshold for a federal Title V operating permit (40 CFR 70). The narrative models 98.28 tons/year NOx; that is not a separate enforceable mass cap. D2 N1 N3 Federal emergency operation has no numerical hour ceiling; maintenance/readiness ordinarily has 100 hours/year, with exceptions; the conditional 50 non-emergency hours are included, not additional. D3 D4 The state data-center pathway specifies <500 total hours/engine/year and routine testing only 10 p.m.–4 a.m., May–September; engines outside that definition face an ozone-season NOx standard. D5 Hour meters, fuel metering or an approved alternative, monthly fuel/hour records and rolling totals are required; reaching the fuel ceiling triggers written notice within 15 days. D6 D9

3. Where it sits in Georgia

This would replace the six-generator issued baseline with a shared fuel limit. N1 It uses the same Title V-avoidance approach as DCB West's 745,286-gallon limit. By contrast, Edged has a direct 99-ton rolling NOx cap. See the 28-facility effective-limit table; these recovered records are not a complete statewide census.

4. Fuel-cap calculation

Using the narrative's 21,042.9 gal/hour campus modeling rate (21,042.9÷103 = 204.3 gal/hour per model generator), 736,501 gallons equals 3,605 aggregate full-load model-generator-hours, or 35 full-campus model hours, if the entire annual allowance remains unused. N12 The model represents even the smaller engines as 3 MW units; actual mixed-fleet outage duration remains unknown. N3 Burning the full capped volume gives about 7,520 metric tonnes CO₂: 736,501×10.21÷1,000, using EPA's stationary No. 2 distillate factor, PDF p.1. This is a cap-based combustion estimate, not expected emissions or a legal CO₂ cap.
